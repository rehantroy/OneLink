Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

What we store

When you sign in with Google, we save your name, email address and profile photo. When you use OneLink, we also save the username and bio you choose, the links you add, and how many times each link was clicked.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data and we don't share it with advertisers. Click counts are just numbers. We don't track who clicked.

What's public

Your public page shows your name, photo, username, bio and the links you have turned on. Hidden links and click counts are only visible to you.

Deleting your data

Email rehantroybusiness@gmail.com from the Gmail you signed in with, and we will delete your account and all your links.
